# Using Places

Places is your list of actually discovered locations, including non-trading sites.
The left navigator chooses an area, the center lists places, and the right panel
shows the current selection. A recorded place is not proof of current stock,
remaining loot or a completed visit.

## Find a place

Choose a system, planetary group or individual body/playfield in the left navigator.
Search by the displayed place name or known alias. Variant letters such as Colony A
come from the known template family, not the order in which you found places.
Same-name places remain separate instances.

Use the list tabs **Favorites**, **Unsorted** and **Hidden** to choose which manual
list to browse. Search and filters apply within that list and geographic scope.
Filter by faction, relation or potential traders. Click the Place, Distance or
Max traders heading to sort; scroll the center list for more results.

Place-name color represents faction color. The separate relation dot represents
your known standing: red Hostile, orange Unfriendly, yellow Neutral or green
Friendly (including Honored). Gray is Unknown; N/A means the relation does not apply.
Hover over the compact filter controls for their labels.

The trader filter offers Any, With traders, Without traders and Unknown traders.
**Up to N traders** is template potential, not a claim about live staff or stock.
No recorded market/request observations means unknown, not that a site has none.

## Browse, check and organize

- Clicking a row only focuses its details. It never checks or unchecks the row.
- Clicking anywhere in the checkbox cell toggles that row once. Other checks stay set.
- Hide and Favorite act on checked rows when any are checked. Otherwise they act on
  the currently focused visible row; an unchecked focused row is not added to a checked set.
- Select all checks the current filtered results. Clear removes checks. Changing
  list, search or filters clears stale checks, so hidden selections cannot be moved accidentally.

Hide moves entries to Hidden; Unhide there returns them to Unsorted. Favorite moves
entries to Favorites; Unfavorite there returns them to Unsorted. Favoriting a hidden
place moves it to Favorites; hiding a favorite moves it to Hidden. Neither action
changes observations, deletes the place or changes native markers. The current list
stays open after an action. Undo restores the previous list memberships.

While a list change is saving, wait for the result. A failure displays an error;
follow its refresh/retry guidance rather than assuming the change was committed.

## Inspect observations

The right panel has **Overview**, **Market** and **Requests**. Select a place for
exact-instance observations, or a navigator area for the observations in that scope.
Market lists distinct offers with item, stock/demand and price. Freshness is shown
through the existing row treatment and hover explanation; different vendor prices
remain separate. Requests includes Pending/Done access.

Use the full-view links for detailed Market or Requests actions; Back returns to the
contextual panel. Compare / Buy A or Sell B sends the selected area or exact place
to that side of Comparison. It does not choose vendors or create a buying plan.

**Visit** explicitly marks the focused place through the existing one-shot visit
marker path when its coordinates are known. Discovering, favoriting or filtering
places does not automatically create a visit marker. Unknown distances or unavailable
Visit actions mean the required location evidence is missing.
